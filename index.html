The flow

12 November
Morning departure for Kagga Kamma. Check into the Cave Suites and self-catering chalets, followed by a sundowner trip and boma dinner.
13-14 November
Slow mornings, stargazing, nature drives, hiking, biking, rock art, pool time, family meals, and proper time together.
15 November
Breakfast, final photos, check-out, and the roadtrip home with one more good padstal stop on the way back.
🛞
Gravel road ready

Leave with a full tank, check the spare tyre, download offline maps, keep water in the car, and take the gravel slowly. Keep distance between cars for dust and loose stones, and aim to arrive in daylight.
