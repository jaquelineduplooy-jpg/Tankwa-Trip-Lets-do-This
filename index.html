The storyline

A birthday weekend with Dad at the heart of it.

This trip starts as a roadtrip, settles into a desert stay, and turns into a birthday story: arrive in the rocks, gather around the table, look up at the stars, and head home feeling properly away.

At the heart of it all is you, Dad, the man of the moment. This weekend is about your stories, your humour, your quiet strength, and the way you have brought this family together over the years.

It is our chance to slow down, raise a glass, share the kind of moments that become family legends, and make sure you know exactly how deeply loved and appreciated you are.
